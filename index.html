Blog Header Image Generator

A wide image that sits above the title and does not fight it.

A header has one job: tell a reader the post is worth opening before they read a word of it. It sits behind or above a headline, so it needs a calm area where the text goes and a single idea somewhere else in the frame. Most stock photos fail at the first part.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate. Nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Ask for 16:9 and say where the empty space goes: "large empty sky on the left for a headline". Name one object and one colour pair. A header with three ideas reads as none.

Leave the words out

Put the title in your CMS, not in the picture. Lettering inside a generated image is the part most likely to come out misspelt, and it cannot be edited later. If the picture itself must carry readable text, GPT Image 2 is the model on the shelf that handles it.
